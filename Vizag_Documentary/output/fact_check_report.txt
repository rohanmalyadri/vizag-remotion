VIZAG — THE COASTLINE BLUEPRINT
FACT-CHECK REPORT
Prepared: 23 November 2026 · Narration: original Telugu voice-over (not altered) · Script: Vizag.pdf

HOW TO READ THIS REPORT
Every claim that became an on-screen graphic was checked against at least one published source.
Status vocabulary used on screen and below:
  CONFIRMED            – documented fact (date/event happened)
  OFFICIAL ANNOUNCEMENT – government statement / press release
  COMPANY COMMITMENT    – investment announced by a company (money not yet spent)
  MoU                   – memorandum of understanding (a promise, not an investment)
  PROPOSED / UNDER CONSTRUCTION / OPERATIONAL – physical status of a project
  COMPANY ESTIMATE / PROJECTED – a forecast by the company or government
  ALLEGATION            – claim by activists / civil-society groups, not independently verified
  POLITICAL CLAIM       – statement by a political party or leader
  GOVERNMENT RESPONSE   – the state's answer to criticism
  UNVERIFIED / DISPUTED – no supporting source found, or sources conflict
The narration was NOT rewritten. Where the narration differs from the record, the on-screen text
shows the verified figure (or avoids the number) and the scene is flagged below with ⚑.
